Asynchronous Optical Sampling Transient Absorption  (ASOPS-TA / AS-TA)

Pump and probe pulse trains run at slightly different repetition rates, fr and fr+Δfr. The detuning Δfr makes the pump–probe delay grow by a fixed step Δt every pulse pair, so the transient-absorption decay is sampled point by point. The whole delay window is swept once every 1/Δfr, after which the identical scan repeats.

Larger Δfr → fewer points per scan (M = fr/Δfr) and a shorter refresh time 1/Δfr, so the delay scan completes and repeats faster. Example: fr=80 MHz, Δfr=800 Hz → M=105, window 1/fr=12.5 ns scanned every 1/Δfr=1.25 ms.
